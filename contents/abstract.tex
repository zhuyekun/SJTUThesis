\begin{abstract}[zh]
  辐射输运问题描述了光子束、质子束等粒子在介质中的传播与能量沉积过程，广泛应用于医学物理放疗剂量计算、核工程辐射防护、大气光学遥感等关键领域。但由于该问题固有的高维相空间特性、积分碰撞项的强耦合性，传统数值方法求解辐射输运方程面临着计算成本随维度指数增长、复杂场景适配难等巨大挑战，难以满足实际工程中大规模的求解需求。本文聚焦于基于深度学习的神经算子方法，系统构建面向稳态辐射输运方程的神经算子方法，实现高精度、高泛化性、高效率的方程求解。

  本文从稳态辐射输运方程的数学模型出发，通过算子分解分析解的结构性质，将求解过程改写为类不动点迭代格式；但其中若干关键算子难以解析获得，因此本文提出一种新型神经网络方法 DeepRTE，以深度神经网络对这些算子进行近似，并通过类残差的迭代式网络结构逼近辐射输运方程的稳态解。该框架在网络架构层面嵌入由辐射输运方程推导得到的物理信息与数学结构，从而在计算效率上优于传统方法与现有神经网络方法；同时引入多头注意力等机制，使模型能够以更少参数刻画高维输入之间的全局相关性并保持高精度；为进一步提升模型的泛化能力，本文结合格林函数理论，将解表示为格林函数的卷积形式，并通过对 Delta 函数入射边界条件的预训练来增强对不同边界设置的零样本（zero-shot）适应性，使 DeepRTE 成为一种无网格的神经算子框架，无需重新训练即可适配全新的边界条件与介质参数组合。

  针对 DeepRTE 在含源项辐射输运场景下，全相空间积分带来的计算复杂度与内存开销激增的核心瓶颈，本文进一步提出高效神经算子 EfficientRTE。该方法基于含源项辐射输运方程格林函数积分解的线性结构，构建编码器 - 解码器的自编码架构，设计 “编码器预训练 - 解码器微调” 的两阶段训练策略：先通过编码器学习源域的低维基函数表示，实现任意体源项的降维编码；再通过解码器学习格林函数的观测域系数，最终将原本需要遍历全相空间的高维积分运算，转化为固定低维的向量线性组合运算。EfficientRTE 在保持与 DeepRTE 相当求解精度的同时，大幅降低了含源项问题的计算复杂度与内存开销，对高斯随机场等复杂空间分布的体源项具备极强的适应能力，拓展了神经算子方法在真实辐射输运场景中的适用性。

  本文基于 JAX 高性能 AI 技术栈，完整实现了 DeepRTE 与 EfficientRTE 的全流程工程化代码库与可复现体系。工程实现中充分利用 JAX 的函数式编程范式、自动微分机制与 XLA 即时编译能力，对衰减模块、散射模块、积分运算等核心物理算子进行了静态图编译优化，大幅降低计算延迟；自主实现了适配两大模型的多 GPU 数据并行策略，实现了训练数据的跨设备分发、梯度的自动聚合与同步；同时完成了多 GPU 并行优化的快速扫掠法，通过角度域并行、空间波前并行与高效集合通信，大幅提升了传统数值解的生成速度，为模型提供了高精度参考解基准与公平的硬件级效率对比标尺。相关预训练模型权重、训练数据集与完整代码已通过平台开源，本文所有算法、数值实验与结果均可通过开源资源完整复现，为后续的扩展研究提供了可靠的工程基础。

  本文的工作系统构建了辐射输运方程的物理约束神经算子求解体系，既通过嵌入辐射输运的物理先验与数学结构，保证了解的物理一致性、可解释性与跨场景泛化性，又通过架构创新与工程优化，突破了传统方法在高维、含源项场景下的计算瓶颈，为医学放疗、核工程等领域的辐射输运仿真提供了高效、可扩展的全新技术路径。

\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}[en]
  The radiative transport problem describes the propagation and energy deposition processes of particles such as photon beams and proton beams in media, and is widely applied in key fields including radiotherapy dose calculation in medical physics, radiation protection in nuclear engineering, and atmospheric optical remote sensing. However, due to the inherent high-dimensional phase space characteristics and the strong coupling of the integral collision term of this problem, traditional numerical methods for solving the radiative transport equation (RTE) face enormous challenges such as exponentially growing computational cost with dimensionality and difficulty in adapting to complex scenarios, which make it hard to meet the large-scale solving requirements in practical engineering. This paper focuses on the deep learning-based neural operator method, and systematically constructs a neural operator framework for steady-state RTE to achieve high-accuracy, strong generalizability, and high-efficiency equation solving.

  Starting from the mathematical model of the steady-state RTE, this paper analyzes the structural properties of the solution through operator decomposition, and reformulates the solving process into a fixed-point-like iterative formulation. Since several key operators in this formulation are difficult to be derived analytically, this paper proposes a novel neural network method named DeepRTE, which approximates these operators via deep neural networks and approximates the steady-state solution of the RTE through a residual-like iterative network structure. This framework embeds the physical information and mathematical structure derived from the RTE at the network architecture level, thus achieving better computational efficiency than both traditional methods and existing neural network methods. Meanwhile, it introduces mechanisms such as multi-head attention, enabling the model to capture the global correlations between high-dimensional inputs with fewer parameters while maintaining high accuracy. To further improve the generalizability of the model, this paper combines Green's function theory to represent the solution in the convolution form of Green's function, and enhances the zero-shot adaptability to different boundary settings through pre-training on Delta-function incident boundary conditions. This makes DeepRTE a mesh-free neural operator framework that can adapt to entirely new boundary conditions and medium parameter combinations without retraining.

  Aiming at the core bottleneck of DeepRTE in source-included radiative transport scenarios, namely the surging computational complexity and memory overhead caused by full phase-space integration, this paper further proposes an efficient neural operator named EfficientRTE. Based on the linear structure of the Green's function integral solution of the source-included RTE, this method constructs an encoder-decoder autoencoder architecture and designs a two-stage training strategy of "encoder pre-training - decoder fine-tuning": first, the encoder learns the low-dimensional basis function representation of the source domain to achieve dimensionality reduction encoding of arbitrary volume source terms; then the decoder learns the observation domain coefficients of Green's function, and finally converts the high-dimensional integral operation that originally requires traversing the full phase space into a fixed low-dimensional vector linear combination operation. While maintaining a solving accuracy comparable to DeepRTE, EfficientRTE greatly reduces the computational complexity and memory overhead of source-included problems, has an extremely strong adaptability to volume source terms with complex spatial distributions such as Gaussian random fields, and expands the applicability of neural operator methods in practical radiative transport scenarios.

  Based on the JAX high-performance AI technology stack, this paper fully implements a full-process engineering codebase and reproducible system for both DeepRTE and EfficientRTE. In the engineering implementation, we make full use of JAX's functional programming paradigm, automatic differentiation mechanism, and XLA just-in-time compilation capability to perform static graph compilation optimization on core physical operators including the attenuation module, scattering module, and integral operations, which greatly reduces the computational latency. We independently implement a multi-GPU data parallelism strategy adapted to both models, realizing cross-device distribution of training data and automatic aggregation and synchronization of gradients. Meanwhile, we complete a multi-GPU parallel-optimized fast sweeping method, which greatly improves the generation speed of traditional numerical solutions through angular domain parallelism, spatial wavefront parallelism, and efficient collective communication, providing a high-precision reference solution benchmark and a fair hardware-level efficiency comparison standard for the models. The relevant pre-trained model weights, training datasets, and complete code have been open-sourced via the platform, and all algorithms, numerical experiments, and results in this paper can be fully reproduced through the open-source resources, providing a reliable engineering foundation for follow-up extended research.

  The work in this paper systematically constructs a physically-constrained neural operator solution system for the radiative transport equation. On the one hand, it ensures the physical consistency, interpretability, and cross-scenario generalizability of the solution by embedding the physical priors and mathematical structure of radiative transport; on the other hand, it breaks through the computational bottlenecks of traditional methods in high-dimensional and source-included scenarios through architectural innovation and engineering optimization, providing an efficient and scalable new technical path for radiative transport simulation in fields such as medical radiotherapy and nuclear engineering.
\end{abstract}
